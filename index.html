What we dance

Rueda de Casino

Philly P'arriba brings Rueda de Casino (Cuban salsa) to the City of Brotherly Love.

Born in Cuba, Rueda de Casino combines rich Latin and Afro-Cuban rhythms with a tradition of community building. Dancers gather in a circle and respond to shared calls, continuously rotating partners as the group moves together.

The result is a whirlwind of rhythm, teamwork, and laughter. Whether you're stepping onto a dance floor for the first time or have years of experience, Rueda offers a fun and welcoming way to connect through music, movement, and community.

Our mission

Dance Community Resistance

Philly P’arriba is committed to creating a welcoming and inclusive community where all people feel safe, valued, and respected. We oppose all forms of discrimination, including racism, antisemitism, Islamophobia, sexism, homophobia, and hostility to immigrants.

As dancers, diversity is our strength, and our lives are enriched by the sounds and traditions of many cultures. Immigrants are a vital part of that experience. Our mission is to use the strength of our connections to demand change for a more just and equitable society.
